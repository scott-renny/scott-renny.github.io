Behind DARKSTAR

Learning by building
things that work.

I wanted an engineering journal and project portfolio that showed more than certifications and screenshots. Most of what you'll find here came from problems I ran into while building and securing my own environment.

I'm working toward cybersecurity and SOC-focused roles, with hands-on work across SIEM, endpoint security, networking, Linux, Windows identity, containers, automation and cloud. I'm also deliberately building toward broader security engineering, cloud security, and AI-assisted automation engineering capability through production-style projects. I don't pretend my homelab is an enterprise environment. Part of the point is learning where the differences are, and what I would do differently at scale.
