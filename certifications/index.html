HOW I USE CERTIFICATIONS

Validate the learning, then build with it.

I don't want certifications to stand alone. The goal is to pair each credential with practical work: cloud deployments, SIEM and endpoint monitoring, identity labs, networking, automation and adversary simulation.

As new certifications are earned, they will move from the roadmap into the verified credential collection above and be connected to the projects that demonstrate the same skills.
