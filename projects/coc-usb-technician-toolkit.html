PERSONAL LAB · WINDOWS SUPPORT ENGINEERING

COC USB #1 — IT Technician Toolkit

Portable support automation, an offline troubleshooting playbook and verified release/rollback workflows.

Problem and approach

A working Ventoy toolkit needed consistent diagnostics, recoverable updates and problem-based guidance without losing existing media, tools or driver backups. I audited the existing assets, reused the collectors and introduced managed reporting and bounded automation.

Project-authored work

  • 28 PowerShell source files, active technician menus and a console-centering helper.
  • Managed collectors, automated/guided sessions, security/enterprise/event/BitLocker diagnostics.
  • Preview-first repair and migration workflows, case baselines/comparison/handover and selective cleanup.
  • Offline playbook with 18 sections, 27 workflows and 62 primary tool/function entries.
  • Shared UCU branding, verified backups and file-integrity evidence.

Validation and limits

The actual USB functional and branding files/backups were hash-verified. Disposable installer success/rollback cases and 12 menu-navigation paths passed. Physical ISO boot tests and representative privileged repair/migration/restore checks remain separate acceptance work. This is personal lab engineering, not a production-readiness or professional employment claim.

Public deliverables

Repository and complete offline playbook ↗ · Engineering journal

Vendor binaries, boot media, driver backups and personal reports are excluded. The published hardware guard intentionally requires configuration for another device.
